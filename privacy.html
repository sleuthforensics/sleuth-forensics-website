Privacy policy

What this website collects, what we do with it, and how to reach us about it.

Sleuth Forensics
Bengaluru, Karnataka, India

Information we collect

We collect only what we need to answer an enquiry and to deliver work that has been agreed:

  • Contact information. The name, email address, phone number, and organisation you choose to enter in the enquiry form. The phone number is optional.
  • Usage data. Aggregate information about how this website is used, collected through Google Analytics. See Cookies and analytics below.
  • Engagement information. Material relating to the scope and conduct of services you ask us to perform, governed by the engagement agreement rather than by this policy.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and correspond with you about our services
  • To deliver, maintain, and improve the services we have been engaged to provide
  • To understand how this website is used and improve it
  • To meet applicable legal obligations

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information, and we do not share it for marketing purposes. Information may be shared with service providers who help operate this website or deliver an engagement, under equivalent confidentiality obligations, and where the law requires disclosure.

Cookies and analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser and processes data, including a truncated form of your IP address, on Google's infrastructure. We use it only for audience measurement, not for advertising, remarketing, or profiling, and we run no advertising or social media tracking scripts.

You can block these cookies through your browser settings or Google's opt-out browser add-on without affecting how this site works. No other cookies are set by this website.

Form submissions

The enquiry form on this site posts to Google Forms, which stores the submission in a Google account controlled by Sleuth Forensics. Submitted material is treated as confidential and is used only to respond to your enquiry.

Data security

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the information we hold, both for website enquiries and, to a considerably greater degree, for engagement material.

Retention

Personal information is retained only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for, or as required by law. Engagement data retention is set out in the relevant engagement agreement.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal information. To exercise them, use the contact route below and we will respond within a reasonable period.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Any revision is published on this page with a new revision date.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to access, correct, or delete your information, should be sent through the enquiry form. Written correspondence can be addressed to Sleuth Forensics Consultancy Services, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.
