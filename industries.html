Industries

Sectors we work in

The method does not change from one industry to the next. What changes is the regulatory context, what an outage actually costs, and which systems cannot be touched during business hours.

01

Technology & SaaS

Product security assessments, infrastructure testing, secure development guidance, and incident response for technology companies and SaaS platforms.

02

Financial services

Security assessment, compliance support, and forensic investigation for organisations handling financial data, payment systems, and regulated operations.

03

Healthcare

Protection of patient data, medical systems, and clinical infrastructure, balancing security controls against the need for continuity of care.

04

Manufacturing

Operational technology security, supply chain risk, and protection of production systems where IT and OT networks meet.

05

Professional services

Cybersecurity support for legal, consulting, and advisory firms that hold sensitive client material and must demonstrate their own security competence.

06

Retail & e-commerce

Payment security, platform assessment, and fraud investigation across online and physical retail environments.

07

Education

Assessment and incident response for institutions running student data, research systems, and open campus networks with diverse user populations.

08

Government & public sector

Assessment, compliance support, and investigation for agencies and public bodies operating under heightened security requirements.

09

Other regulated industries

Insurance, logistics, energy, and other sectors with regulatory obligations or high-value data. The approach adapts to the risk context rather than the label.
