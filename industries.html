Industries We Serve

We work with organisations across industries where cybersecurity, data protection, digital investigations, and operational resilience are priorities.

Industry sectors

Technology & SaaS

Product security assessments, infrastructure testing, secure development guidance, and incident response support for technology companies and SaaS platforms. We help technology organisations identify vulnerabilities before they are exploited and build security into their development lifecycle.

Financial Services

Security assessments, compliance support, and forensic investigation services for organisations handling financial data, payment systems, and regulated financial operations. We understand the regulatory environment and security requirements specific to financial services.

Healthcare

Protection of patient data, medical systems, and healthcare infrastructure through security assessments, compliance support, and incident response. Healthcare organisations face unique challenges in balancing security with accessibility and patient care continuity.

Manufacturing

Operational technology security, supply chain risk assessment, and infrastructure protection for manufacturing environments. We help manufacturers address the convergence of IT and OT security and protect against threats to production systems.

Professional Services

Cybersecurity support for legal firms, consulting companies, and advisory practices that handle sensitive client information and need to demonstrate security competence to their own clients. Protecting client data is fundamental to professional services.

Retail & E-Commerce

Payment security, platform security assessments, and fraud investigation for online and physical retail environments. We help retail organisations protect customer data, payment card information, and e-commerce platforms from targeted attacks.

Education

Security assessments and incident response for educational institutions managing student data, research systems, and distributed campus environments. Educational organisations face unique challenges from diverse user populations and open network architectures.

Government & Public Sector

Security assessments, compliance support, and investigation services for government agencies and public-sector organisations. We understand the heightened security requirements and regulatory frameworks applicable to public-sector environments.

Other Regulated Industries

Cybersecurity services for organisations in insurance, logistics, energy, and other sectors with regulatory requirements or high-value data. Regardless of industry, our approach adapts to the specific risk context and compliance needs of your organisation.
