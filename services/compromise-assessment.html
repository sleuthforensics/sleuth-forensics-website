Compromise Assessment & Threat Hunting

Proactive examination of your environment to identify indicators of compromise, persistent threats, and unauthorised activity that may have evaded your existing detection capabilities.

Overview

Not all compromises are immediately apparent. Sophisticated attackers can maintain persistent access for extended periods, and existing security controls may not detect all forms of unauthorised activity. A compromise assessment proactively hunts for evidence of current or past intrusions across your environment.

This service is particularly valuable when an organisation suspects compromise but has no confirmed indicators, or when there is a need to verify that no undetected threats exist in the environment.

Who This Is For

  • Organisations that suspect compromise but lack confirmed evidence
  • Companies seeking periodic threat hunting as a proactive measure
  • Organisations going through mergers, acquisitions, or significant transitions
  • Companies with concerns about insider threats
  • Organisations that have not previously conducted a thorough compromise review

What We Examine

  • Endpoint telemetry and forensic artefacts
  • Network traffic patterns and anomalies
  • Authentication logs and access patterns
  • Known threat actor techniques, tactics, and procedures
  • Persistence mechanisms and scheduled tasks
  • Unusual process execution and service configurations
  • Indicators of compromise from threat intelligence sources
  • Email and communication artefacts

What You Receive

  • Compromise assessment report with findings and evidence
  • Indicators of compromise identified during the assessment
  • Threat hunting analysis and methodology documentation
  • Recommendations for detection improvement
  • Escalation support if active compromise is confirmed
