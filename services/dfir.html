Digital Forensics & Incident Response

When security incidents occur, our DFIR practice combines forensic rigour with operational response to investigate what happened, determine scope, support containment, and guide recovery while preserving relevant evidence.

Overview

Digital Forensics and Incident Response (DFIR) brings together two complementary disciplines: the structured, evidence-based analysis of forensic investigation, and the operational urgency of incident response. This combination ensures that response actions are informed by evidence, and that evidence is preserved even as containment and recovery activities proceed.

Our DFIR engagements help organisations understand what happened, how it happened, what was affected, and what needs to be done to contain the threat and recover securely.

Who This Is For

  • Organisations experiencing active security incidents
  • Companies that have discovered indicators of compromise
  • Organisations requiring post-incident forensic investigation
  • Legal and compliance teams needing evidence-backed investigation findings

What We Do

  • Rapid forensic triage and evidence collection
  • Scope determination and affected system identification
  • Attacker activity reconstruction and timeline analysis
  • Containment guidance and eradication support
  • Malware and artefact analysis
  • Root cause identification
  • Recovery guidance and security improvement recommendations
  • Stakeholder communication support

Our Approach

DFIR engagements begin with understanding the current situation, establishing priorities, and defining the most critical questions that need to be answered. We work alongside your internal teams to collect and analyse evidence, provide containment recommendations, and guide recovery activities.

Throughout the process, we maintain clear communication, document findings as they emerge, and adapt our investigation as new evidence is identified.

What You Receive

  • Incident investigation report with findings and evidence
  • Complete timeline of attacker activity
  • Scope assessment identifying affected systems and data
  • Root cause analysis
  • Containment and recovery recommendations
  • Post-incident improvement recommendations
