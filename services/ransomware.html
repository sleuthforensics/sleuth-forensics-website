Ransomware Investigation & Readiness

Prepare your organisation for ransomware threats through readiness assessments, and support investigation and recovery when ransomware incidents occur.

Overview

Ransomware represents one of the most significant operational risks for organisations today. Our ransomware services address both preparedness and response: helping organisations assess their readiness for ransomware scenarios, develop response playbooks, and provide expert support when ransomware incidents occur.

Who This Is For

  • Organisations seeking to assess and improve ransomware readiness
  • Companies experiencing active ransomware incidents
  • Leadership teams concerned about ransomware risk to operations
  • Organisations developing ransomware response playbooks

What We Cover

  • Ransomware readiness assessment and gap analysis
  • Backup and recovery capability evaluation
  • Active ransomware incident investigation
  • Ransomware variant identification and analysis
  • Scope of encryption and impact determination
  • Recovery planning and secure restoration guidance
  • Response playbook and tabletop exercise development
  • Post-incident improvement recommendations

What You Receive

  • Ransomware readiness assessment report (proactive engagements)
  • Incident investigation report (reactive engagements)
  • Recovery and restoration guidance
  • Response playbooks and decision frameworks
  • Security improvement recommendations
