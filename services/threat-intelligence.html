Threat Intelligence & Attack Surface Monitoring

Monitor your external attack surface, identify exposed assets and leaked credentials, and contextualise threat intelligence relevant to your organisation and industry.

Overview

Understanding your external exposure and the threat landscape relevant to your organisation is essential for proactive security. Our threat intelligence and attack surface monitoring services help organisations identify what is visible to potential attackers, track relevant threats, and take informed action to reduce exposure.

Who This Is For

  • Organisations seeking visibility into their external attack surface
  • Security teams looking to operationalise threat intelligence
  • Companies concerned about credential leaks and exposed data
  • Organisations in industries frequently targeted by specific threat groups

What We Cover

  • External attack surface discovery and mapping
  • Exposed asset identification (domains, IPs, services, cloud resources)
  • Leaked credential monitoring
  • Dark web and underground forum monitoring
  • Brand impersonation and phishing detection
  • Threat actor tracking relevant to your industry
  • Vulnerability intelligence and prioritisation

What You Receive

  • Attack surface assessment report
  • Exposed asset inventory and risk assessment
  • Threat intelligence briefings relevant to your organisation
  • Monitoring alerts and notifications
  • Remediation recommendations for identified exposure
