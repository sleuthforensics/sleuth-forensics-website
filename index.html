01 · Position

Evidence first. Everything we report is something we observed, recorded, and can show you.

Sleuth Forensics works with organisations across India to assess security controls, test for vulnerabilities, investigate suspicious activity, respond to incidents, and build resilience over time.

Assessment work is manual as well as automated, because the findings that change a decision are rarely the ones a scanner prints. Investigation work is documented as evidence, because sooner or later somebody senior will ask how you know.

03 · Method

How an engagement runs

Six phases, in order. The scope changes from one engagement to the next; the sequence does not.

01

Understand

Establish the environment, the business context, and the specific concern that prompted the engagement. Scope follows from this, not the other way round.

02

Examine

Review systems, configurations, controls, evidence, and telemetry. Manual work where it matters, tooling where it scales.

03

Analyse

Correlate findings across sources, separate noise from real risk, and establish what an issue would actually mean in your environment.

04

Report

Set out the evidence, severity, business impact, and prioritised recommendations, in one document that reads at both technical and board level.

05

Improve

Work through remediation with your team: practical guidance and a roadmap that accounts for what you can realistically change.

06

Validate

Retest the fixes. A finding is closed when we can show it is closed, not when a ticket is marked done.

05 · Approach

What we hold to

Evidence over assumption

Every finding states what was observed, where it was found, and why it matters. If we cannot show it, we do not report it.

Depth beyond the scanner

Automated tools open the engagement, they do not close it. Business logic flaws, chained weaknesses, and attack paths are found by hand.

Written for two audiences

The same report has to work for the engineer who will fix the issue and the director who has to fund the fix. We write both parts properly.

Confidentiality by default

Assessments and investigations surface things organisations would rather not have in circulation. Engagement data is handled accordingly.
