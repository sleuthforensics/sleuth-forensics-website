Our Approach

A structured, repeatable methodology

Every engagement follows a consistent process designed to maximise thoroughness, minimise disruption, and produce findings you can act on immediately.

01

Understand

Understand the environment, business objectives, risk context, and specific concerns that shape the engagement scope.

02

Examine

Review systems, configurations, controls, evidence, and telemetry through manual and structured analysis techniques.

03

Analyse

Correlate findings across data sources, determine meaningful risk, and establish the real-world impact of identified issues.

04

Report

Present clear evidence, severity ratings, business impact, and prioritised recommendations in structured deliverables.

05

Improve

Provide practical remediation guidance and an improvement roadmap that your team can implement systematically.

06

Validate

Where applicable, verify that implemented improvements have addressed the identified issues and reduced exposure effectively.
