About Sleuth Forensics

A cybersecurity services and digital forensics firm that helps organisations understand their security exposure, investigate what matters, and build practical resilience.

Our Mission

Security improvement through evidence and clarity

Sleuth Forensics was founded on the principle that effective cybersecurity depends on understanding what is actually happening in your environment, not just what might theoretically go wrong.

We combine technical security assessment with investigative capability to provide organisations with a clear picture of their security posture, the evidence to support informed decisions, and practical guidance to improve their defences.

Our work spans preventive security, such as assessments and testing, reactive investigation when incidents occur, and strategic advisory to build organisational resilience over time.

What guides us

Evidence over assumption

Every finding is supported by evidence. We present what we observed, where we found it, and why it matters.

Clarity in communication

Technical findings are only useful if they can be understood and acted upon. We prioritise clear reporting for both technical and non-technical audiences.

Practical improvement

Our recommendations account for your organisation's operational reality. We focus on improvements that can be implemented, measured, and sustained.

Confidentiality and trust

Security engagements involve sensitive information. We maintain strict confidentiality and handle all data with appropriate care.

Our Approach

How we work

Every engagement follows a structured methodology designed to maximise thoroughness, minimise disruption, and produce findings your organisation can act on.

01

Understand

Understand the environment, objectives, risk context, and specific concerns that shape the engagement scope.

02

Examine

Review systems, configurations, controls, evidence, and telemetry through manual and structured analysis.

03

Analyse

Correlate findings, determine meaningful risk, and establish the real-world impact of identified issues.

04

Report

Present clear evidence, severity ratings, business impact, and prioritised recommendations.

05

Improve

Provide practical remediation guidance and an improvement roadmap for systematic implementation.

06

Validate

Verify that improvements have addressed the identified issues and reduced exposure effectively.
